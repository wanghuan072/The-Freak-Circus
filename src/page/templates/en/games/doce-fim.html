Doce Fim

Doce Fim

As the world hurtles towards its predicted end, 'Doce Fim' throws you into a deeply personal narrative where the apocalypse serves as a backdrop for exploring the intricacies of human connection. It's not just about surviving; it's about finding meaning, love, and friendship amidst the chaos. This isn’t just a game; it’s a journey of self-discovery and the enduring power of art and connection in the face of oblivion.

About

'Doce Fim' is a choice-driven narrative game that delves into the themes of queer love, platonic friendship, and the redemptive power of art. Set against the backdrop of a world supposedly nearing its end, the game focuses on the protagonist's internal struggles and their relationships with others. Over a decade in the making, 'Doce Fim' explores the complexities of trust, paranoia, and the search for meaning in a seemingly hopeless situation. The game features a unique 'Duality Mode,' which challenges players to balance positive and negative impulses, influencing character relationships and shaping the narrative. The TRUST system adds another layer of depth, where choices affect character trust levels, unlocking unique dialogues and leading to varied endings. With its emphasis on accessibility and inclusivity, 'Doce Fim' offers a thought-provoking and emotionally resonant experience for all players, highlighting the importance of connection and resilience even in the darkest of times.

Features

  • 300k Words of Narrative: Immerse yourself in a rich and detailed story that offers over 30 hours of gameplay, filled with meaningful choices and character interactions.
  • Multiple Routes & Endings: Experience four unique routes, including romantic and platonic options, each with good and bad endings determined by your choices, adding replayability and encouraging exploration. A True Route exists for those who delve deeper.
  • Duality Mode: Navigate challenging situations using the protagonist's internal positive and negative sides, each offering different perspectives and influencing your available choices.
  • TRUST System: Build or break trust with other characters through your decisions, unlocking unique bonding conversations and potentially leading to dramatically different outcomes.
  • Paranoia Bubbles: Experience the protagonist's anxiety and paranoia through visual representations that distort reality, adding a layer of psychological depth to the narrative.
  • Post-Route Insights: Gain a deeper understanding of other characters' true thoughts and feelings by unlocking updated Paranoia Bubbles after completing their route.
  • Accessibility Options: Customize your experience with audio descriptions, self-voicing, adjustable text font and size, and options to disable specific audio elements, ensuring inclusivity for all players.
  • Partial Voice Acting: Enjoy enhanced immersion with partial voice acting for the main cast, bringing the characters and their emotions to life.

FAQ

  • What is the core theme of 'Doce Fim'?
    'Doce Fim' primarily explores themes of queer love, friendship, and the power of art to create meaning and connection in a world facing its end. It is about finding hope and resilience in the face of adversity.
  • How does the Duality Mode work?
    The Duality Mode presents choices influenced by the protagonist's positive and negative sides. Picking options stemming from negativity will increase distrust, while choices based on positivity might lead to naivety. Balancing these aspects is key to navigating the game.
  • What is the significance of the TRUST system?
    The TRUST system tracks your relationships with other characters. High trust unlocks unique bonding conversations, revealing deeper aspects of their personalities. Low trust can lead to unfavorable outcomes and potentially bad endings.
  • What are Paranoia Bubbles?
    Paranoia Bubbles are visual representations of the protagonist's anxious and paranoid thoughts. They distort your perception of reality, adding a layer of psychological depth to the gameplay. After completing a route, these bubbles get updated to reveal the true thoughts and motivations of the character involved.
  • Is 'Doce Fim' accessible to players with disabilities?
    Yes, 'Doce Fim' offers a range of accessibility options, including audio descriptions, self-voicing, adjustable text font and size, and the ability to disable certain audio elements to cater to different needs.
  • How many different endings are there?
    Each of the four main routes (one romantic, two platonic, and the True Route) features a good and a bad ending. The outcome you achieve depends on the choices you make throughout the game.

'Doce Fim' is more than just a game; it's an exploration of what it means to be human in the face of overwhelming uncertainty. With its compelling narrative, unique gameplay mechanics, and focus on inclusivity, it offers a deeply moving and thought-provoking experience that will stay with you long after the credits roll.
