Get in Touch

We'd love to hear from you! Whether you have questions about our services, suggestions for improvement, or just want to say hello, we're here to help.

Contact Information

Email:wyong@thefreakcircus.org

We typically respond within 24 hours.

Response Time

  • Monday - Friday: Within 24 hours
  • Weekends: Within 48 hours

What We Can Help With

  • Account or Access Issues: Help with login, membership, or subscription problems
  • Website Feedback: Suggestions for improving The Freak Circus
  • Content Requests: Ideas for new articles, guides, or resources
  • Technical Support: Assistance with site functionality
  • Partnership Inquiries: Collaboration opportunities
  • General Questions: Anything else related to The Freak Circus and our services

Before You Contact Us

To help us provide the best assistance, please include the following details in your email:

  • Your specific question or issue
  • Any error messages (if applicable)
  • Steps you've already tried
  • Your device and browser information (if relevant)

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How often is your content updated?
    We update our resources regularly to ensure accuracy and freshness.
  • Is your content safe and reliable?
    Yes, all information we provide is verified and sourced responsibly.
  • Can I suggest new content?
    Absolutely! We welcome your ideas and contributions—just send them our way.

Business Hours

Our team is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (EST). While we may respond outside these hours, please allow up to 24–48 hours for replies during weekends and holidays.

Privacy

We respect your privacy and will never share your personal information with third parties. All communications are kept confidential and used only to provide you with the best possible service.

Thank You

Thank you for being part of the The Freak Circus community! Your feedback and support help us continue to provide the best experience for our users worldwide.
