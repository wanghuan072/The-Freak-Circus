Lookouts

Amidst the arid landscapes and simmering tensions of the Old West, 'Lookouts' presents a story of two outlaws, both trans masc, who unexpectedly find common ground. This visual novel delves into the complexities of identity, belonging, and the pursuit of a better life against the backdrop of a harsh and unforgiving world. Get ready to immerse yourself in a narrative rich with emotion, adventure, and the blossoming of an unexpected romance. This 5-6 hour experience is a must play!

About

'Lookouts' is more than just a romance; it's a heartfelt exploration of the human condition. Set in the Old West, it tells the story of two gay trans masc outlaws whose paths cross in the desert. Far from their respective gangs, they discover a shared sense of isolation and a mutual yearning for something more. Drawn together by their circumstances and a shared desire for a different life, they venture towards a town rumored to hold gold, a symbol of hope and opportunity. The narrative sensitively portrays their struggles, their growing connection, and their resilience in the face of adversity. With approximately 45,000 words, 'Lookouts' offers a deeply engaging and emotionally resonant experience for players.

FAQ

  • What is 'Lookouts' about?
    'Lookouts' is a romance visual novel set in the Old West, focusing on two gay trans masc outlaws who find love and hope amidst a harsh and unforgiving landscape.
  • How long does it take to play?
    The game offers roughly 5-6 hours of reading time, providing a substantial and immersive narrative experience.
  • What are the content warnings?
    The game contains depictions of alcohol, smoking, gun violence (with SFX), gun death, blood, injury, mild transphobia, and discussions of racism and settler violence. Please consider these warnings before playing.
  • Is 'Lookouts' a commercial game?
    'Lookouts' is available to play online; it was initially developed for the Gay Western Jam and has since been expanded.
  • Where can I play the original jam version?
    The original jam version is linked on the game's page for those interested in experiencing the initial concept.
