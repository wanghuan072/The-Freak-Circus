Heartstop Tour

Ready to ditch the mundane and dive headfirst into a chaotic, hilarious, and potentially romantic adventure? About

In Heartstop Tour, you're not just playing a character; you're living a rom-com movie. After a particularly insulting remark from your boss regarding your beloved childhood mascot, Comfort Kuma, you impulsively quit. Now, armed with nothing but a sad sandwich and a questionable suit, you find yourself mistaken for a tour guide by a ridiculously charming (and utterly clueless) tourist. This is where your heartstop journey truly begins. But that's not all! A desperate attempt to recapture some childhood joy leads you to a fateful encounter with the real Comfort Kuma, or rather, the unbelievably cute guy hiding inside the pink bear suit. Prepare for parade accidents, misplaced cameras, and a whole lot of awkward but endearing moments. Your tour through love is about to get very interesting. Are you ready for this heartstop situation?
